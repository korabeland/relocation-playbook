# Job-Search

This folder is only a boundary. It is not a job-search application and the kit includes no job-search tools.

If one of you is searching for work during the move, that search is an individual matter. Keep its detail here, or in your own area, and out of the shared plan. Only the effects on the move enter shared planning: timing, income, and coverage.

How it works in practice:
- Put pipeline notes, company research, and interview notes in this folder only within the owner-only workspace, or in another place only you can see. Never share this folder or its parent workspace with the household.
- Mark any related entries in `state/` as `visibility: private`, so they are left out of shared agendas and summaries.
- When something changes the shared picture, such as a start date, a gap in income, or a change in coverage, record that effect as a fact or a deadline with `visibility: household`. Leave the details of how it came about out of it.
- In `PLAN.md`, record the constraint, for example "income gap from <date>", not the search.

The folder starts empty on purpose.
