# Documents

Outputs that the people moving with you will read or use: checklists, comparison tables, drafts of letters, packing lists, budget workbooks, and the next-session agenda if you keep it as a file.

Rules for this folder:
- Draft anything meant for another household member here, in plain language. This folder stays owner-only. Share only reviewed, approved copies in a separate household output location.
- Documents are created by the document workflow (`workflows/document.md`). They are drafts until you review them.
- The assistant does not send, submit, sign, or file anything. You do that yourself, outside the assistant.
- Do not keep identity scans, signed tax documents, or other original records here unless you have decided to share them with the household. Sharing with a household is not publishing.
- Starting structures for a budget and a household sale list are in `examples/`. Copy them into your private instance and fill them in there.

Note the path `Documents/<filename>` on the related task page when you create or update a document.
