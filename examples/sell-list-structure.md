# Sale list structure

`sell-list-structure.csv` is an empty table with a header row. Use it to plan selling household items before a move. Copy it into your private instance and fill it in there.

## Columns

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `item` | What it is |
| `group` | `sell-now`, `sell-later`, or `sell-last` (see below) |
| `list_by` | The date you will put it up for sale |
| `hand_over_by` | The date the buyer collects it |
| `details` | Condition, size, notes for the listing |
| `status` | `not-listed`, `listed`, `sold`, `handed-over`, or `donated` |

## The two dates matter

Listing date and handover date are different. Things you use until the last day, such as a bed or a fridge, should be listed early with a late handover, because they take weeks to sell. If you wait to list them until you stop using them, you run out of time.

## Three timing groups

- `sell-now`: things you do not need before the move, and slow sellers.
- `sell-later`: secondary items that you can part with a few weeks before departure.
- `sell-last`: daily essentials that go in the final days.

Add a backstop for what does not sell, such as a donation pickup or a council collection, and write down its date.

## Limits

An assistant can help you draft listings and keep the table tidy. Do not expect it to write into a shared spreadsheet automatically. The original setup could not, and the table was handed over by pasting it in. Check whether yours can before you rely on it.

Keep prices, items, and pickup details of your own out of anything you share.
