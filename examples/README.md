# Examples

Everything here is invented. No person, place, date, or figure comes from a real household.

- `synthetic-relocation/`: a small made-up household with a filled-in plan, state records, a session record, and a research file. Read it to see what the records look like once they are used.
- `budget-structure.md`: how to structure a relocation budget in four buckets, with scenarios. It is a design explanation, not a spreadsheet file.
- `sell-list-structure.csv` and `sell-list-structure.md`: a blank schema for a household sale list, and an explanation of its columns and timing groups.

These show shape. They are not advice for any country, and the amounts and rules in them are placeholders.
