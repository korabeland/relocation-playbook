# Shipping versus selling household furniture

Researched: 2031-01-18
Situation assumed: Two adults and a cat, moving from Country A to Country B on 2031-03-15. Whether to ship by sea or air is not decided. Quotes are not yet in. The household has a rental at the destination for the first month only.

## Summary

Large, low-cost furniture is usually cheaper to sell and replace than to ship. Small, hard-to-replace, and sentimental items usually justify shipping. This is a rule of thumb, and the quotes decide it. Everything here is invented for this example.

## Recommendation

Option 2, pending three quotes at different volume levels. Collect the quotes before listing anything large. Revisit if the middle quote is far above the planning range.
