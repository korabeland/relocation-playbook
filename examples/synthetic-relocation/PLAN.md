# Relocation plan (synthetic example)

Invented for illustration. Last reviewed: 2031-01-18

## 1. Situation

- Who is moving: Alex and Riley, with one cat.
- Moving from: Country A, a mid-sized city.
- Moving to: Country B, a coastal town.
- Why now: Riley has a role starting there and Alex plans to work remotely.
- Must preserve: continuous health coverage, a pet transport plan, and Alex's current contract until the end of March.
- Flexible: how much furniture comes along, and the first month's housing type.

## 2. Timing model

**Date-led.** Departure on 2031-03-15, with travel booked. Backstop: 2031-04-05, after which the rental at the destination would need renegotiating.

On 2030-12-02 the plan switched from window-led to date-led once the travel was booked. The open tasks were re-dated at that point.

## 3. Constraints

| Area | Constraint | Source | Last checked |
|---|---|---|---|
| Work and income | Alex's contract runs until 2031-03-31 and allows remote work from abroad for 60 days | Contract, read 2030-12-05 | 2030-12-05 |
| Health coverage | Current plan ends on departure. A bridge is needed for the first three months | Provider notice | 2030-12-10 |
| Money | A six-month runway is the minimum before departure. Figures kept in the private budget | Agreed in session | 2031-01-04 |
| Immigration and identity | Riley's residence permit is issued. Alex's is pending | Placeholder status | 2031-01-12 |
| Housing | Notice on the current home is 60 days. Short-term rental booked for the first month | Lease and booking | 2031-01-10 |
| Family | The cat needs a transport booking and a vet certificate | Airline policy | 2031-01-12 |

## 4. Phases and triggers

| Phase | Meaning | Enters when |
|---|---|---|
| A Foundation | Research and no-regret paperwork | Always open |
| B Ready | Prepared so decisions can be acted on quickly | Skipped in this date-led plan |
| C Hold | Skipped | Not used |
| D Locked | Tasks back-planned from the departure date | Travel booked on 2030-12-02 |
| E Landing | Arrival and first weeks | Departure day |

| Trigger | Type | What it activates |
|---|---|---|
| Alex's permit decision arrives | Soft Lock | Remote-work paperwork and the bridge coverage start date |
| Travel booked | Hard Lock | All D Locked tasks (already happened) |

## 5. Responsibilities

- Alex: permits, coverage, pet transport.
- Riley: housing, banking, the new role's onboarding.
- Both: shipping versus selling decision, budget.

## 6. Weekly rhythm

- Shared review: Sundays, 45 minutes, both attend. Starts with session start.
- Solo sessions: any time. They close with session end too.
- Weekly summary: Sunday evening, for both.
