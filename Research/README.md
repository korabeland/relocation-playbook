# Research

One file per researched question, saved as `Research/<area>/<topic>.md` using `templates/research-file.md`. Suggested areas: `admin`, `finance-tax`, `healthcare`, `shipping`. Add your own.

Rules for this folder:
- Research is dated and tied to one situation. Each file states the date and the circumstances it assumed.
- Rules, fees, and eligibility change. Treat a file as a lead to recheck, not as settled advice, especially once your circumstances change. Old research that predates a change in your plan needs a second look.
- Prefer official and primary sources, and say when a source is not one.
- Research files are written by the research workflow (`workflows/research.md`). Questions waiting to be researched live in `state/research-queue.yaml`.
- Keep third-party forms and signed documents out. Link to the official page instead.

This folder is private. It will contain details of your situation.
