# Task page template

Use this as the body of every task page on the task board. The board's fields (Phase, Status, Owner, Trigger, Category, Due) hold the structured data. The body holds the detail.

```markdown
## What
[What needs to happen, and why it matters to the move.]

## Next actions
- [ ] Step one
- [ ] Step two

## Research
[Findings, or a link to the file in Research/. The assistant fills this in.]

## Progress log
- YYYY-MM-DD: note
```

Keep the progress log short and dated. When a task moves to Waiting, the first line of the log says what it is waiting for.
