# Weekly summary template

Save a summary as `Weekly/YYYY-MM-DD.md`. It should be readable in two minutes by someone who was not in the room. Compose it from `state/` and the task board, not from memory of the conversation.

```markdown
# Weekly summary: YYYY-MM-DD

## Finished
- [Task]: [one-line note]

## Underway
- [Task]: [what is happening, and any blocker]

## Next two weeks
- [Task]: [why now]

## Decisions needed
- [Decision]: [context, and options if they are known]

## New research
- [Topic]: [main finding, and the path to its file in Research/]
```

Only include entries with `visibility: household` unless the reader is the person the private entry belongs to.
