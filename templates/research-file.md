# Research file template

Save each research result as one file at `Research/<area>/<topic>.md`, using this layout. Research is tied to one situation on one date. Write both down.

```markdown
# [Topic]

Researched: YYYY-MM-DD
Situation assumed: [the facts about us this answer depends on, and where they are recorded]

## Summary
[Two or three sentences with the main finding.]

## Options

### Option 1: [Name]
- Pros:
- Cons:
- Cost:
- Source: [link and the date you read it]

### Option 2: [Name]
- Pros:
- Cons:
- Cost:
- Source:

## Recommendation
[What to do next, and what would change the recommendation.]

## What is uncertain
[Anything not confirmed, anything that depends on a rule that may change, and who or what could confirm it.]

## Follow-ups
- [Fact to add to state/facts.yaml, deadline to add, decision to open, or task to create. These are proposals until session close.]
```

Notes for the assistant:
- Prefer official and primary sources. Say when a source is a forum post or a commercial site.
- Do not present a recommendation as a confirmed fact.
- Do not give legal, tax, or medical advice as settled. Name the professional or official body that can confirm.
